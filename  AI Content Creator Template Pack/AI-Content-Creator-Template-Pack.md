# AI CONTENT CREATOR TEMPLATE PACK

## Create Better Content Faster With AI

A practical collection of AI prompts designed to help creators generate ideas, hooks, captions, scripts, and content plans in minutes.

---

# 1. VIRAL CONTENT IDEAS

### Prompt 1 — Generate Content Ideas

```text
Act as an expert social media content strategist.

My niche is: [NICHE]

My target audience is: [AUDIENCE]

Give me 20 content ideas that:
- Solve real problems
- Are easy to understand
- Encourage engagement
- Have strong potential for short-form video
- Do not require expensive equipment

For each idea, provide:
1. Content topic
2. Hook
3. Main talking points
4. Call to action
```

### Prompt 2 — Find Trending Angles

```text
Act as a viral content strategist.

My niche is [NICHE].

Give me 15 fresh angles I can use to talk about this niche without copying other creators.

Make the ideas:
- Interesting
- Unexpected
- Useful
- Easy to turn into TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube Shorts

Rank the 5 strongest ideas first.
```

# 2. VIRAL HOOKS

### Prompt 3 — Generate Strong Hooks

```text
Create 30 powerful hooks for content about [TOPIC].

Use different hook styles:
- Curiosity
- Controversy
- Questions
- Mistakes
- Surprising facts
- Storytelling
- Strong opinions

Keep each hook short enough to say in the first 3 seconds of a video.
```

### Prompt 4 — Improve My Hook

```text
Here is my current hook:

[PASTE HOOK]

Rewrite it into 10 stronger versions.

Make them:
- More attention-grabbing
- Natural
- Short
- Curiosity-driven
- Suitable for TikTok/Reels
```

# 3. SHORT-FORM VIDEO SCRIPTS

### Prompt 5 — 30-Second Video

```text
Act as a professional short-form video scriptwriter.

Create a 30-second video script about [TOPIC].

Structure it like this:

HOOK:
Grab attention immediately.

PROBLEM:
Explain the problem.

VALUE:
Give the viewer useful information.

CTA:
Tell the viewer exactly what to do next.

Make the script conversational and easy to say naturally.
```

### Prompt 6 — Storytelling Video

```text
Turn this idea into an engaging short-form story:

[IDEA]

Use this structure:

1. Attention-grabbing opening
2. Set the scene
3. Introduce the problem
4. Build curiosity
5. Reveal what happened
6. Lesson
7. Call to action

Keep it under 45 seconds.
```

# 5. PRODUCT PROMOTION

### Prompt 9 — Sell Without Sounding Pushy

```text
Act as a social media marketing expert.

I sell: [PRODUCT]

My target customer is: [CUSTOMER]

Create 10 social media posts that promote my product without sounding desperate or overly sales-focused.

Focus on:
- Problems
- Benefits
- Results
- Customer needs
- Curiosity

End each post with a soft CTA.
```

### Prompt 10 — Product Video Script

```text
Create a 20-second promotional video script for:

PRODUCT: [PRODUCT]
TARGET CUSTOMER: [CUSTOMER]
MAIN BENEFIT: [BENEFIT]

Structure:

0-3 seconds: Hook
3-8 seconds: Problem
8-15 seconds: Solution
15-18 seconds: Benefit
18-20 seconds: CTA

Make it sound natural and modern.
```

# 9. CONTENT IMPROVEMENT

### Prompt 14 — Content Critic

```text

Analyze this content:

[PASTE CONTENT]

1. What is good about it?
```

## QUICK START

Replace anything inside `[BRACKETS]` with your own information.

### Example:

Instead of:

`My niche is: [NICHE]`

Use:

`My niche is: men's fashion`

Instead of:

`My target audience is: [AUDIENCE]`

Use:

`My target audience is: men aged 18-30 who want affordable fashion`

Then paste the prompt into your preferred AI tool.
