# QUICK START

Replace anything inside `[BRACKETS]` with your own information.

### Example:

`PRODUCT: [PRODUCT]`

becomes:

`PRODUCT: Classic white sneakers`

`TARGET CUSTOMER: [CUSTOMER]`

becomes:

`TARGET CUSTOMER: Men and women aged 18–30 who want affordable casual footwear`

The more accurate your information, the more useful the AI output can become.
