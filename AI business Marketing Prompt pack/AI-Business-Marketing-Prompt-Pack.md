# AI BUSINESS & MARKETING PROMPT PACK

## 50 AI Prompts to Help You Start, Market & Grow a Business

A practical collection of AI prompts designed to help entrepreneurs save time, generate ideas, understand customers, create marketing content, and improve their businesses.

---

# 1. BUSINESS IDEAS

## Prompt 1 — Generate Business Ideas

```text
Act as an experienced entrepreneur and business strategist.

I want to start a business.

My skills are: [SKILLS]
My interests are: [INTERESTS]
My budget is: [BUDGET]
My location/target market is: [LOCATION]

Give me 15 realistic business ideas.

For each idea include:
1. What the business does
2. Target customer
3. Problem it solves
4. How it makes money
5. Startup difficulty
6. How I could get my first 5 customers
```

## Prompt 2 — Find Problems Worth Solving

```text
Act as a business opportunity researcher.

My target audience is: [AUDIENCE]

List 20 common problems, frustrations, or inconveniences this audience experiences.

For each problem:
- Explain why it matters
- Suggest a possible solution
- Explain how a business could make money solving it
```

# 2. CUSTOMER RESEARCH


```text

My business:

[DESCRIBE BUSINESS]

[DESCRIBE CUSTOMER]

[DESCRIBE CHALLENGE]

[DESCRIBE GOAL]

2. Why it matters

4. The best solution
```

Replace anything inside `[BRACKETS]` with your own information.

`Business: [BUSINESS]`

`Business: Online clothing store`

`Target customer: Women aged 18–30 who want affordable fashion`

The more specific information you provide, the more useful the AI response can become.
