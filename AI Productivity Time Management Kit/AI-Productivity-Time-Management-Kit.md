# ⚡ AI Productivity & Time Management Kit

## Work Smarter. Save Time. Get More Done.

This kit gives you ready-to-use AI prompts for planning your day, managing tasks, building routines, staying focused, beating procrastination, organizing projects, and improving productivity.

You can copy any prompt, replace the information inside `[brackets]`, and paste it into ChatGPT or another AI assistant.

# 💡 BONUS: Productivity Rules

### Rule 1

**Don't confuse being busy with being productive.**

### Rule 2

**Start with the highest-value task.**

### Rule 3

**Break large tasks into smaller actions.**

### Rule 4

**Make your environment support your goals.**

### Rule 5

**Use AI to plan and organize, but you still have to execute.**

### Rule 6

**A realistic plan you follow is better than a perfect plan you abandon.**
