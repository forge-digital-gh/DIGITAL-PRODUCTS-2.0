# 🏆 THE 10 AI RULES

## 1. Give Context

AI performs better when it understands your situation.

## 2. Give a Clear Goal

Tell AI what you actually want.

## 3. Give Constraints

Mention budget, time, audience, skill level, or other limitations.

## 4. Give Examples

Show AI what you like when possible.

## 5. Ask for Structure

Tell AI whether you want a table, checklist, plan, script, etc.

## 6. Don't Accept Everything Blindly

AI can make mistakes.

## 7. Verify Important Information

Especially when decisions involve money, health, law, safety, or current facts.

## 8. Add Your Personality

AI should assist your thinking, not replace your identity.

## 9. Iterate

Your first prompt doesn't have to be perfect.

## 10. Execute

The best AI strategy is useless if you never take action.
