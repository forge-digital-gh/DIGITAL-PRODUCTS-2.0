# PART 31 — AI QUALITY CONTROL

Never automatically trust an AI answer.

Before using important information ask:

* Is the information accurate?
* Does it require current data?
* Can I verify it?
* Did AI make an assumption?
* Did AI invent anything?
* Is the recommendation appropriate for my situation?
* What could go wrong?
* What information is missing?

For important decisions, verify critical facts independently.
