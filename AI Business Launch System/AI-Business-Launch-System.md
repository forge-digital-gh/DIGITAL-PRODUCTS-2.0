# START HERE

Welcome to the **AI Business Launch System**.

This system helps you turn a business idea into a practical launch plan using AI.

It is designed for:

* Beginners
* Entrepreneurs
* Small businesses
* Online businesses
* Local businesses
* Service businesses
* E-commerce businesses
* Creators
* Solopreneurs

The goal isn't to create a complicated business plan that sits in a folder.

The goal is to build a business that can actually be tested in the real world.

# PART 25 — BUSINESS DECISION FILTER

Before making a major decision, ask:

### 1. Does this help the customer?

### 2. Does this solve a real problem?

### 3. Does this improve the business?

### 4. What evidence supports the decision?

# PART 26 — GOLDEN RULES

## Rule 1

Don't fall in love with the idea.

Fall in love with solving the customer's problem.

## Rule 2

Don't build everything before testing demand.

Test first.

## Rule 3

Don't compete only on price.

Find a meaningful reason customers should choose you.

## Rule 4

Don't chase every marketing channel.

Master the channels where your customers actually are.

## Rule 5

Don't confuse activity with progress.

Measure results.

## Rule 6

Don't use AI to invent facts.

Verify important information.

## Rule 7

Don't promise customers unrealistic results.

Build trust through useful outcomes.

## Rule 8

Start small.

Learn.

Improve.

Then scale.
