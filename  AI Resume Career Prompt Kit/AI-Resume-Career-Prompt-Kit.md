# AI RESUME & CAREER PROMPT KIT

## 50 AI Prompts to Help You Build a Stronger Resume, Prepare for Interviews & Plan Your Career

A practical collection of AI prompts designed to help job seekers, students, graduates, and professionals improve their career materials and prepare more effectively for opportunities.

---

# 1. RESUME / CV CREATION

## Prompt 1 — Create a Resume

```text
Act as a professional resume writer.

My information:

Name: [NAME]
Target role: [JOB TITLE]
Education: [EDUCATION]
Experience: [EXPERIENCE]
Skills: [SKILLS]
Certifications: [CERTIFICATIONS]
Achievements: [ACHIEVEMENTS]

Create a professional, clear, ATS-friendly resume.

Do not invent experience, qualifications, or achievements.

Use strong action verbs and focus on measurable results where information is available.
```

## Prompt 2 — Improve My Resume

```text
Act as an expert resume editor.

Here is my current resume:

[PASTE RESUME]

Review it and identify:

1. Weak sections
2. Unclear wording
3. Repetitive information
4. Missing important details
5. Formatting/content problems
6. Opportunities to make achievements stronger

Then provide an improved version.

Do not add information that I did not provide.
```

## Prompt 3 — Tailor My Resume

```text
Act as an ATS resume specialist.

Here is my resume:

[PASTE RESUME]

Here is the job description:

[PASTE JOB DESCRIPTION]

Tailor my resume to this specific role.

Identify relevant keywords from the job description and naturally incorporate them where they accurately match my experience.

Do not lie or invent qualifications.
```

[COMPANY OR INDUSTRY]

## Prompt 13 — Mock Interview

```text
Act as my interviewer for a [JOB TITLE] position.

Ask me one interview question at a time.

After each answer:
1. Score my answer from 1–10
2. Explain what I did well
3. Explain what I could improve
4. Give me a stronger example answer

Then ask the next question.
```

# QUICK START

Replace anything inside `[BRACKETS]` with your own information.

### Example:

Instead of:

`Target role: [JOB TITLE]`

Use:

`Target role: Junior Web Developer`

Instead of:

`Skills: [SKILLS]`

Use:

`Skills: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python`

The more accurate information you provide, the more useful the AI output can become.
