# PART 29 — GOLDEN RULES

## Rule 1

Don't chase every trend.

Only use trends that make sense for your audience.

## Rule 2

A strong hook gets attention.

A strong idea keeps attention.

Useful content builds trust.

## Rule 3

Don't confuse views with business results.

Track the metric connected to your actual goal.

## Rule 4

Don't copy another creator's identity.

Study what works and develop your own voice.

## Rule 5

One strong idea can become many pieces of content.

Learn to repurpose.

## Rule 6

Consistency beats unsustainable posting schedules.

Build a system you can maintain.

## Rule 7

AI should accelerate creativity, not replace your judgment.

Always review important content before publishing.
