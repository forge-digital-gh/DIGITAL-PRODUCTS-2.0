# FINAL RULES

1. Don't choose products purely because they're trending.
2. Validate before investing heavily.
3. Understand the customer.
4. Focus on problems and outcomes.
5. Don't invent product claims.
6. Don't fake reviews.
7. Don't use fake scarcity.
8. Make the buying process simple.
9. Make shipping information clear.
10. Make returns understandable.
11. Track your numbers.
12. Test before making large changes.
13. Improve the customer experience.
14. Build repeat customers.
15. Protect your profit margins.
16. Use AI to speed up work, not replace judgment.
