# QUICK START

Replace information inside `[BRACKETS]` with your own business details.

### Example:

Instead of:

`BUSINESS: [BUSINESS]`

Use:

`BUSINESS: Online clothing store`

Instead of:

`TARGET CUSTOMER: [CUSTOMER]`

Use:

`TARGET CUSTOMER: Women aged 18–30 looking for affordable fashion`

The more specific your information is, the more useful the AI output can become.
