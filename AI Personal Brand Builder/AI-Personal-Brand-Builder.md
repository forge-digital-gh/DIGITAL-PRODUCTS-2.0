# PART 19 — BRAND QUALITY CHECK

Before publishing content, ask:

* Is this useful?
* Is it relevant to my audience?
* Does it sound like me?
* Does it support my positioning?
* Is it easy to understand?
* Does it provide a clear takeaway?
* Is the hook honest?
* Is the CTA appropriate?
* Does it build trust?
* Would I be proud for someone to associate this with my name?

If the answer is mostly yes, publish.

# PART 21 — PERSONAL BRAND DECISION FILTER

Before doing something, ask:

### 1. Does it help my audience?

If no → reconsider.

### 2. Does it strengthen my positioning?

If no → reconsider.

### 3. Does it build trust?

If no → reconsider.

### 4. Does it move me toward my goal?

If no → reconsider.

### 5. Can I do it consistently?

If no → simplify.

# PART 22 — GOLDEN RULES

1. Be useful before trying to go viral.
2. Be recognizable before trying to be everywhere.
3. Build trust before aggressively selling.
4. Don't pretend to know what you don't know.
5. Your story is part of your brand.
6. Your audience matters more than follower count.
7. Consistency beats random bursts of activity.
8. Study what works, but don't copy someone's identity.
9. Build proof through real work.
10. Turn knowledge into systems.
11. Turn systems into content.
12. Turn content into relationships.
13. Turn relationships into opportunities.
14. Turn opportunities into results.
15. Turn results into stronger proof.
